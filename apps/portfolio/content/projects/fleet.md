Fleet is a digital freight forwarder. As a senior front-end engineer I built the customer-facing rate,
booking and shipment tracking views in Vue on top of a Rails API, in a code-review-driven agile workflow
with a small team.

## Highlights

- Instant quoting and booking flows for ocean and air freight
- Component and view tests with Jest
- Occasional devops for the front-end build and deploys
