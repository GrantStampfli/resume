Koho is Expeditors' digital less-than-truckload platform. I worked across the full stack, but the bulk of
my time went into the Vue application: a shared component library, an instant-rate quoting flow, and a
booking experience that had to stay fast while talking to several carrier APIs.

## Highlights

- Built the Vue component library used across the product
- Test-driven approach that kept UI regressions out of production
- Worked directly with design, product and stakeholders on the user experience
